Core loops

Four loops, four speeds

The fast loops are small on purpose. Each one feeds the slower one above it, and the slowest one ends with her needing the app less.

Loop one

The day

The daily loop happens mostly away from the phone. The app's part is one question in the morning and one tap after the anchor. It closes by telling her she is finished.

  1. CueSomething she already does
  2. TouchHow much do I have?
  3. RepThe anchor, out in her life
  4. SavorTen seconds to feel it
  5. GlimmerOne small beautiful thing
  6. A dayTen seconds
What she gets backA trail that wears in, and a day sized to her energy.
Why she returnsIt asks almost nothing, and it never punishes a gap.

Loop two

A hard moment

This loop is not scheduled. It is there when the evening goes sideways. Every pass through it is a rep on the new path, whatever she ends up choosing.

  1. TriggerHer feminine hour arrives
  2. PauseShe taps the centre button
  3. RitualBreath or body
  4. NoticeName the loudest thought
  5. ChooseWhat matters to her, next
  6. A momentNinety seconds
What she gets backA gap between the urge and the action.
Why she returnsIt worked last time, and nothing in it told her off.

Loop three

A moon

This is the loop that carries accountability. The new moon turns intention into a plan with the obstacle already named. The full moon turns two weeks of small data into one decision.

  1. SetWish, obstacle, plan, word
  2. WitnessOne person knows
  3. CardOn the fridge, off the phone
  4. Live itTouches and pauses
  5. GatherWhat grew, ungraded
  6. AdjustStay, move on, or rest
  7. A moonTwo weeks
What she gets backEvidence of who she is becoming, and a say in what comes next.
Why she returnsThe moon keeps the appointment, and a friend is going to ask.

Loop four

A season

The longest loop is the point of the whole thing. As anchors stop needing effort they retire, the daily list gets shorter, and the physical things take over the remembering. If life changes and an old pattern returns, she comes back round, and the circle starts at rest again.

  1. Old pathThe pattern she got through on
  2. New pathRest, rebuild, rise, release
  3. Second natureAnchors leave the list
  4. Need it lessCard, deck and kin carry it
  5. A seasonMonths
What she gets backA way of living that no longer depends on the app.
Why she returnsOnly when a season turns, or when she wants to.

Together

How they feed each other

Day into moonTen-second touches are the data. Without them the gathering has nothing to gather.
Moment into AlmanacEach Pause teaches the Almanac her feminine hour, so care can arrive before the hard minute does.
Moon into seasonThe gathering is where a phase ends, an anchor retires, or the plan shrinks to fit a hard stretch.
Season into dayAs anchors become second nature, the daily list gets shorter until it is almost nothing.

Accountability

What holds her to it, without streaks

Nothing in the app counts consecutive days. Four other things do the holding.

  • The mirror

    The Almanac hands her own patterns back. Curiosity brings her in more reliably than guilt does.

  • The witness

    One person holds her word and asks about it at the full moon.

  • The calendar

    New and full moon are fixed nights. Nobody has to decide when to check in.

  • The thing she can hold

    A moon card on the fridge and a deck on the table remind her without a screen.
