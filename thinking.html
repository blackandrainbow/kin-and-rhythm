The thinking

The ideas underneath

Six ideas hold the product together. None of them is new on its own. The work was in making them agree with each other.

1

Start with energy, not food

Most plans ask a mother to change how she eats while she is running on empty, and then blame her when it does not last. A depleted body defaults to whatever is easiest. So the first thing the app asks is what fills her cup, and the first phase of every path is rest.

Food arrives later, and only as addition: something stirred into the pot she already makes. Nothing is taken off her table.

2

A pattern is a season she is in

Under long stress, people get through in recognisable ways: pushing harder, staying in motion, looking after everyone else, running out, or going quiet. The app names five of these as weather, never as personality, because a state can change and an identity feels fixed.

She is shown her two or three closest matches and chooses for herself. The names, words and pictures are original to this project.

  • SunPushing through

    The path: cool and soften.

  • WindStaying in motion

    The path: land and ground.

  • HearthKeeping everyone warm

    The path: refill and hold some back.

  • TideRunning on empty

    The path: warm and rebuild.

  • FrostGone quiet

    The path: thaw and wake.

3

Recovery is a circle

Each path moves through four phases (rest, rebuild, rise, release) and then comes round again. Nobody graduates and nobody fails. She can go back to rest whenever she needs to.

The same four turn up inside her month as inner winter, spring, summer and autumn, following her cycle, or the moon when she has none. A good day in inner winter looks different from a good day in inner summer, and the app plans accordingly.

4

Rewiring, honestly

Becoming a mother is one of the largest changes an adult brain goes through, which is proof enough that it can change. The app treats her old pattern as a well-worn path and the plan as a new one, worn in by small reps tied to things she already does, and by stopping for ten seconds to feel each one.

It stays honest about the science. There is no promise of a new habit in twenty-one days and no claim that one phase of her cycle is better for learning. A missed day is treated as what it is: nothing.

5

Kindness as the method

The coaching approach comes from acceptance and commitment practice: know what matters to her, notice thoughts without obeying them, and take the next small step toward what matters.

Shame is left out because it does not work. That is why there are no streaks, why every option in Pause is allowed, and why the app names her own values back to her at the hardest moment of the day.

6

Things to hold

The deck, the moon card and the printed grid exist so she can put the phone down and still remember. A card on the fridge does not send notifications.

The hope written into the product is that one day the card is enough.

Limits

What it does not claim

Kin & Rhythm is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat anything. It makes no promises about hormones, supplements or weight.

This is a prototype. The screens are real and the flows work, but the demo moms and their history are invented, and nothing entered leaves the device it was typed on.
